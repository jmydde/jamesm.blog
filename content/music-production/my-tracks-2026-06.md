A selection of my music production work from June 2026.

June was the UK Garage month. Almost every idea got at least one garage rework, and a lot got two - the Future Garage and UKG mixes of the same song became a running theme. There are piano house edits, dream mixes, club mixes, and a midnight run of Velvet versions to close things out.

You can find more of my work on [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/jamesm).
