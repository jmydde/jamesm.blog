A selection of my music production work from May 2026.

May found me chasing textures and contrasts: shakuhachi tones against pulsing synth lines, disco strings over garage swing, and a handful of remixes that rework older ideas into new moods. The J-Pop trance flavour crept in too, and the month closed with a couple of Feel The Thunder reworks.

You can find more of my work on [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/jamesm).
