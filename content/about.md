I'm James M - a Staff Data Engineer by trade, and this is where the rest of it goes.

## Why this blog exists

I read and build across a wider range of things than any one job calls for, and most of it had nowhere to live. This is that place: long-form essays where I've actually worked through an argument, resource pages I keep coming back to myself, and monthly notes on what I'm building, listening to, or trying to understand better.

I write about:

- [**Artificial Intelligence**](/ai/) - LLMs, agents, and what's actually changing versus what's just noise
- [**Data Engineering**](/data-engineering/) and [**Data Science**](/data-science/) - the infrastructure and the day job
- [**DevOps**](/devops/) and [**Software Engineering**](/software-engineering/) - systems, tooling, and the craft of building things that hold up
- [**Security**](/security/) - how systems actually get broken, and how to design against it
- [**Blockchain**](/blockchain/) - decentralized infrastructure, on its merits, without the hype
- [**Music Production**](/music-production/) - gear, synthesis, and the tracks I make in whatever time is left
- [**Retro Computing**](/retro-computing/) - the machines and culture that shaped how I think about building software at all
- [**Space**](/space/) and [**Gaming**](/gaming/) - infrastructure and economics at very different scales
- [**Personal Development**](/personal-development/) - expertise, craft, and staying honest about both
- [**Theories**](/theories/) and [**General**](/general/) - physics, cosmology, and the big open questions, written as a hobbyist, not a physicist

## The stance

On the technical posts, I try to write what I'd actually want to read: grounded in sources, honest about uncertainty, and willing to say "I don't know" instead of manufacturing a confident take. On the physics and consciousness posts especially, I'm an outsider with a strong interest and no formal training - I say so in every one of them, and I'd rather be corrected than sound more certain than I am.

I use AI tools extensively in researching and drafting this blog. I verify claims, check links, and edit for my own voice and views before anything publishes - but I'd rather be upfront about the process than pretend otherwise.
