## TL;DR

- **Anthropic** released **Claude Opus 4.6** and **OpenAI** released **GPT-5.3-Codex**, both on 5 February
- Anthropic announced a **$30 billion Series G at a $380 billion valuation** on 12 February
- **Google** released **Gemini 3.1 Pro** in preview on 19 February
- **OpenAI** announced **$110 billion** of financing on 27 February, which grew to $122 billion by the end of March

Benchmark and capability claims below come from the companies unless noted.

## Claude Opus 4.6 and GPT-5.3-Codex (5 February)

The two biggest coding-model launches of the month landed the same day.

**Claude Opus 4.6.** Anthropic's [SiliconANGLE coverage](https://siliconangle.com/2026/02/05/anthropic-rolls-claude-opus-4-6-1-million-token-context-support/) describes a 1 million token context window (in beta), adaptive reasoning and agent teams in Claude Code, at unchanged pricing of $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens. Anthropic says it improves long-running agentic work and reliability in large codebases, and that it beats GPT-5.2 and Gemini 3 Pro on its tests, which are Anthropic's own claims.

**GPT-5.3-Codex.** [OpenAI's announcement](https://www.openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-3-codex) presents it as moving from a coding assistant towards an agent that can operate a computer for professional work. Coverage such as [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/gpt-5-3-codex-openai-agentic-ai-launch/) reports it runs about 25% faster than the previous generation. OpenAI said it was the first of its models to be instrumental in creating itself, and the first it classifies as "high capability" for cybersecurity under its Preparedness Framework. A smaller real-time variant, GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark, followed in research preview.

## Anthropic's $30 Billion Series G (12 February)

[Anthropic announced](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-raises-30-billion-series-g-funding-380-billion-post-money-valuation) a $30 billion Series G at a $380 billion post-money valuation, led by GIC and Coatue with D. E. Shaw Ventures, Dragoneer, Founders Fund, ICONIQ and MGX co-leading. The company put its run-rate revenue at $14 billion and said Claude Code's run-rate was above $2.5 billion. Crunchbase called it the largest venture deal of 2026 to that point. The valuation roughly doubled the September 2025 Series F, though sources quote slightly different figures for that earlier round.

## Gemini 3.1 Pro (19 February)

Google released Gemini 3.1 Pro in preview across the Gemini API, AI Studio, Vertex AI, the Gemini app and NotebookLM, according to the [Google Cloud announcement](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/gemini-3-1-pro-on-gemini-cli-gemini-enterprise-and-vertex-ai) and [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/02/19/google-introduces-gemini-3-1-pro-model-advanced-reasoning-tasks/). Google reported a verified score of 77.1% on ARC-AGI-2, which it said was more than double Gemini 3 Pro's reasoning performance. Those figures come from Google.

## OpenAI's $110 Billion Financing (27 February)

OpenAI announced $110 billion of financing on 27 February, according to [Verdict](https://www.verdict.co.uk/openai-122bn-funding-round/), which reported it was aimed at a $730 billion pre-money valuation. The round grew and closed at $122 billion on 31 March, covered in the [March roundup](/ai/ai-roundup-march-2026/).
