## TL;DR

- **OpenAI** launched **GPT-5.4** on 5 March, its first general-purpose model built to operate desktop computers
- OpenAI announced on 24 March that it is **shutting down Sora**, the app and the API
- OpenAI closed a **$122 billion round at an $852 billion post-money valuation** on 31 March

March was dominated by OpenAI. Performance claims come from the company unless noted.

## GPT-5.4 (5 March)

OpenAI launched GPT-5.4, with Thinking and Pro variants for ChatGPT and Codex and a Mini and Nano pair following on 17 March. According to [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/openai-launches-gpt-5-4/), [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/03/05/openai-launches-gpt-5-4-computer-vision-tool-use-enhancements/) and [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/03/05/openai-new-model-gpt5-4-enterprise-agentic-anthropic/):

- It is positioned as OpenAI's first general-purpose model with native computer use
- OpenAI says it makes 33% fewer individual false statements and 18% fewer fully erroneous responses than GPT-5.2
- It costs more per token than its predecessor, and GPT-5.4 Pro is priced at $30 input and $180 output per million tokens
- GPT-5.2 Thinking stays available to paid users until its retirement on 5 June

## OpenAI Shuts Down Sora (24 March)

OpenAI announced on 24 March that it is ending the Sora video app and its developer API, six months after launching the standalone app. As [Engadget](https://engadget.com/ai/openai-is-shutting-down-its-sora-video-generation-app-211023358.html) and [OPB](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/03/24/openai-pulls-the-plug-on-sora-the-viral-ai-video-app-that-sparked-deepfake-concerns) reported:

- The announcement came from the Sora account and gave no reason
- Reporting points to the cost of serving video and a shift towards business and coding tools ahead of a possible stock market listing
- Disney's planned $1 billion investment in OpenAI was reported to have collapsed
- OpenAI said it would publish timelines for winding down. One site gave specific dates but I could not confirm them elsewhere, so I am not repeating them

## OpenAI Closes $122 Billion (31 March)

OpenAI announced it had closed a funding round with $122 billion of committed capital at an $852 billion post-money valuation. According to [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/03/31/openai-just-closed-record-breaking-122b-funding-round-brings-value-852b/) and [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-122-billion-round-852-billion-valuation-retail-investors):

- Amazon, Nvidia and SoftBank anchored the round, with Amazon's commitment of up to $50 billion the largest and Nvidia and SoftBank at $30 billion each
- Almost $3 billion came from individual investors through bank channels
- OpenAI said it was generating about $2 billion of revenue a month and is not yet profitable
- It topped the $110 billion financing announced on 27 February
