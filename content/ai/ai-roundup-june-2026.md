## TL;DR

- **Anthropic** announced on 1 June that it had **confidentially submitted a draft S-1** to the SEC for a proposed IPO
- Anthropic **expanded Project Glasswing** on 2 June
- **Claude Fable 5** launched on 9 June as the first publicly available "Mythos-class" model, alongside the restricted **Claude Mythos 5**

June was an Anthropic-heavy month. Performance and safety claims come from the company unless noted.

## Anthropic Files for an IPO (1 June)

Anthropic said it had confidentially submitted a draft registration statement to the SEC. According to [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2184970/anthropic-is-set-to-go-public-after-filing-paperwork-with-the-sec) and [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-ipo-confidential-filing-openai-race-965-billion):

- The filing came days after the $65 billion Series H at a $965 billion valuation
- Anthropic said the offering depends on market conditions and that the price and share count were not yet set
- Coverage framed it as a race with OpenAI, which was reported to be preparing its own confidential filing

The IPO later moved into the public stage, covered in the [October](/ai/ai-roundup-october-2026/) and [November](/ai/ai-roundup-november-2026/) roundups.

## Glasswing Expands (2 June)

[9to5Mac reported](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/02/anthropic-expands-glasswing-as-it-promises-public-claude-mythos-class-model-releases/) that Anthropic broadened participation in Project Glasswing, its trusted-access cybersecurity program, and promised public releases of Mythos-class models.

## Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 (9 June)

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5 and announced Claude Mythos 5. According to [Constellation Research](https://www.constellationr.com/insights/news/anthropics-claude-fable-5-mythos-5-what-you-need-know), [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/anthropic-claude-mythos-fable-5/) and [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/anthropic-claude-fable-5-safety-fallbacks/):

- **Fable 5** is the first publicly available Mythos-class model, sitting above Opus in capability, available through the Claude API, Amazon Bedrock and paid plans at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens
- **Mythos 5** is limited to select cybersecurity and life-sciences organisations through Project Glasswing, and is described as the same underlying model with fewer restrictions
- In high-risk domains, Fable 5 falls back to Opus 4.8. One report said this happens without notification, which drew criticism
