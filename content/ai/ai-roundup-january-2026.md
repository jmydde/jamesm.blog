## TL;DR

- **Apple and Google** announced a multi-year partnership on 12 January: Gemini models will underpin the next generation of Siri and Apple Intelligence
- **Moonshot AI** released **Kimi K2.5** on 27 January, a roughly one-trillion-parameter open-weight model
- **Meta** guided to **$115 billion to $135 billion** of capital expenditure for 2026, mostly to build AI infrastructure

This roundup covers what major outlets reported. Benchmark and capability claims come from the companies unless noted.

## Apple Chooses Gemini for Siri (12 January)

Apple and Google announced a multi-year partnership under which Google's Gemini models will be folded into Apple Foundation Models, powering a redesigned Siri and new Apple Intelligence features due later in the year. As [Engadget reports](https://engadget.com/ai/apples-siri-ai-will-be-powered-by-gemini-153636649.html), the companies gave few details. [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/apple-siri-google-gemini-deal-3631890) also covered the announcement.

- The announcement came almost a year after Apple delayed the AI-powered Siri it first promised at WWDC 2024
- An Apple spokesperson said the deal does not change its partnership with OpenAI
- Alphabet's market value briefly passed $4 trillion for the first time, according to coverage
- Financial terms were not disclosed. A figure of about $1 billion a year circulated on a blog but I found no confirmation from established outlets, so I am not repeating it

## Kimi K2.5 Goes Open-Weight (27 January)

Moonshot AI released Kimi K2.5, a one-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 32 billion active parameters, under a modified MIT licence with weights on Hugging Face. [Caixin](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2026-01-28/moonshot-ai-gets-more-into-agents-with-new-model-102408981.html) and [TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/moonshot-ai-launches-kimi-k2-5-with-swarm-of-100-parallel-agents) describe its focus on agents, including a "swarm" mode that coordinates many parallel agents, and vision-based coding. Moonshot also released a coding tool, Kimi Code, that works with editors such as VS Code and Cursor.

Moonshot says it is its strongest open-source model for coding with vision, and press coverage reported it scoring close to leading US models. Those are company and press claims rather than independent results.

## Meta's Capex Guidance (28 January)

With its fourth-quarter results Meta guided to 2026 capital expenditure of **$115 billion to $135 billion**, up from about $72 billion in 2025. According to [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/meta-stock-climbs-on-q4-earnings-beat-plans-to-spend-as-much-as-135-billion-on-ai-build-out-in-2026-154456872.html) and [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/earnings/26/01/50216913/metas-ai-2026-capex-can-pay-for-90-nfl-stadiums-10-james-webb-telescopes-and-rivals-new-yorks-budget-as-zuckerberg-bets-big-on-superintelligence), the spending is for data centres, servers and the costs of large-scale AI development, tied to Meta Superintelligence Labs. The stock rose sharply after the report, and the guidance came in above analyst estimates.
