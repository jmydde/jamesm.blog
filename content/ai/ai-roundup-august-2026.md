## TL;DR

- **Demis Hassabis** stepped back as CEO of Google DeepMind on 5 August, becoming chairman, with **Koray Kavukcuoglu** taking over day-to-day running
- **Alibaba** released **Qwen3.8-Max** on 3 August and **xAI (now SpaceXAI)** released **Grok 4.6** on 12 August
- **Anthropic** reported Q2 revenue above $11.5 billion and a first positive operating result
- **OpenAI** cut GPT-5.6 prices and made **GPT-5.6 Luna** the default for free ChatGPT users

Benchmark claims below come from the companies unless noted.

## Hassabis Steps Back at Google DeepMind (5 August)

As [Axios reported](https://axios.com/2026/08/05/google-deepmind-demis-hassabis-ai) and [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2231124/google-deepmind-ceo-demis-hassabis-is-stepping-down-but-will-remain-with-the-company/) confirmed, Demis Hassabis is leaving the CEO role at Google DeepMind to become the unit's chairman, while remaining with Alphabet. Coverage adds that he becomes Alphabet's chief scientist and stays CEO of Isomorphic Labs, and that Koray Kavukcuoglu, previously DeepMind's chief technology officer, takes over day-to-day operations reporting to Sundar Pichai. Several outlets reported that chief scientist Jeff Dean and some colleagues are leaving to start a company in which Google will invest. Titles vary slightly between outlets, so check Google's own statement for exact wording.

## Qwen3.8-Max (3 August)

Alibaba released Qwen3.8-Max, its largest model so far, a mixture-of-experts model with 2.4 trillion total and 95 billion active parameters and a context window of up to one million tokens. According to [Alibaba Group](https://www.alibabagroup.com/document-2021044032125272064) and [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/alibaba-qwen38-max-most-capable-model), it is available through Alibaba Cloud's Model Studio, with open weights planned. Alibaba's own benchmark claims have not been independently verified.

## Grok 4.6 (12 August)

xAI, now described by some outlets as SpaceXAI, released Grok 4.6, focused on long-running agents. According to [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/spacexai-debuts-grok-4-6-overtaking-kimi-k3s-performance-and-matching-gpt-5-6-sol-for-worlds-third-best-on-artificial-analysis) and [xAI](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-6), it is available in Cursor, Grok Build and the xAI API. Comparative benchmark scores are largely self-reported.

## Anthropic's Second Quarter

Anthropic told prospective investors that Q2 revenue exceeded $11.5 billion, more than 14 times the prior year, and reported its first positive operating result, according to [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-q2-2026-revenue-11-5-billion-operating-income). The exact profit figure differs between sources, so treat any single number as unconfirmed. The detailed figures arrived in the company's public IPO filing, covered in the [November roundup](/ai/ai-roundup-november-2026/).

## OpenAI Cuts Prices

OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Luna's API price by 80% and Terra's by 20% on 30 July, according to [Constellation Research](https://www.constellationr.com/insights/news/openai-cuts-price-gpt-56-luna-gpt-56-terra) and [BleepingComputer](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/openai-says-its-new-gpt-56-models-are-becoming-more-cost-efficient), taking Luna to $0.20 per million input tokens and $1.20 per million output tokens. In early August, coverage reports it became the default model for free ChatGPT users, with unlimited text chats.
