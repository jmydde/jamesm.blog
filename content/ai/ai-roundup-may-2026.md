## TL;DR

- **Google** announced **Gemini 3.5 Flash** and the **Gemini Spark** agent at I/O on 19 May
- **Anthropic** announced a **$65 billion Series H at a $965 billion post-money valuation** on 28 May, more than doubling its February valuation and passing OpenAI's reported valuation

May was a quieter month for frontier model launches. Performance claims come from the companies.

## Google I/O: Gemini 3.5 Flash and Gemini Spark (19 May)

At I/O 2026 Google announced Gemini 3.5 Flash as the first model in the Gemini 3.5 family. According to [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2176592/google-says-gemini-3-5-flash-rivals-large-flagship-models-for-coding-and-agentic-tasks), [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/artificial-intelligence/story/google-io-2026-google-announces-gemini-3-5-models-and-gemini-spark-ai-agent-532351-2026-05-19) and [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/article/google-debuts-gemini-35-ai-model-family-at-io-conference-174500727.html):

- Gemini 3.5 Flash became the default model in the Gemini app and in AI Mode in Search worldwide
- Google says it beats Gemini 3.1 Pro on several coding and reasoning benchmarks and rivals larger flagship models for coding and agentic tasks. Those are Google's claims
- Gemini 3.5 Pro was said to be coming in June
- **Gemini Spark**, a personal AI agent running on 3.5 Flash, began rolling out to testers, initially for AI Ultra users in the US
- Other announcements included a video model and work on smart glasses

## Anthropic Raises $65 Billion (28 May)

[Anthropic announced](https://www.anthropic.com/news/series-h) a $65 billion Series H at a $965 billion post-money valuation, led by Altimeter Capital, Dragoneer, Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital. Coverage from [Crunchbase](https://news.crunchbase.com/ai/anthropic-nears-1t-valuation-65b-seriesh/) and [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/claude-company-anthropic-nears-a-trillion-dollar-valuation-after-raising-65-billion-in-series-h/) adds:

- Anthropic said its run-rate revenue crossed **$47 billion** earlier in the month
- The valuation was $380 billion at the February Series G
- Sources differ on how much is new money: Anthropic's announcement includes $15 billion of previously committed hyperscaler investment, while Crunchbase put the new capital at about $50 billion
- The valuation exceeded OpenAI's $852 billion, though OpenAI had raised roughly twice as much in its round

The company filed confidentially for an IPO days later, covered in the [June roundup](/ai/ai-roundup-june-2026/).
