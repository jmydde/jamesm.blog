## TL;DR

- **Anthropic** announced **Project Glasswing** and **Claude Mythos Preview** on 7 April, a cybersecurity-focused model it chose not to release publicly
- **Meta** launched **Muse Spark** on 8 April, its first proprietary, closed-weight model
- **Anthropic** released **Claude Opus 4.7** on 16 April
- **OpenAI** released **GPT-5.5** on 23 April

Performance claims below come from the companies unless noted.

## Project Glasswing and Claude Mythos Preview (7 April)

Anthropic announced [Project Glasswing](https://www.anthropic.com/project/glasswing), an effort to secure critical software powered by its most capable model, Claude Mythos Preview. According to [HPCwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/aiwire/2026/04/09/anthropic-unveils-project-glasswing-as-claude-mythos-targets-software-vulnerabilities/) and other coverage:

- The model was **not released publicly**. Access went to a consortium of partners, with coverage naming companies including Apple, Google, Microsoft, Amazon, CrowdStrike, NVIDIA and JPMorganChase, though lists vary by outlet
- Anthropic committed up to $100 million in usage credits and $4 million in donations to open-source security organisations
- Anthropic said it had used the model internally to find thousands of zero-day vulnerabilities across major operating systems and browsers. That is Anthropic's claim

## Meta Launches Muse Spark (8 April)

Meta Superintelligence Labs released Muse Spark, a multimodal reasoning model, and unlike the Llama line its weights are not public. As [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/goodbye-llama-meta-launches-new-proprietary-ai-model-muse-spark-first-since) and [Quartz](https://qz.com/meta-muse-spark-ai-superintelligence-labs-040826) reported, it is available through the Meta AI app and a private API preview. Meta says it hopes to open-source future versions but has committed to nothing. Benchmarks were described as competitive rather than dominant, and Meta's stock rose more than 9% on the day.

## Claude Opus 4.7 (16 April)

Anthropic released Claude Opus 4.7, which became the default Opus model across Claude products. I covered it in [Claude Opus 4.7: Autonomy and Vision at Scale](/ai/claude-opus-4-7/).

## GPT-5.5 (23 April)

OpenAI released GPT-5.5, calling it "a new class of intelligence". [OpenAI's announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-5/) and [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/04/23/openai-releases-gpt-5-5/) report:

- It rolled out to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise and to Codex, with API access following on 24 April
- It arrived about six weeks after GPT-5.4 and is priced higher
- OpenAI reports state-of-the-art results across 14 benchmarks. These are vendor-reported figures
- It went through pre-deployment testing under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework, including targeted red-teaming for cybersecurity and biology
