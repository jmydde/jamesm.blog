## TL;DR

- On 12 November 2026 Nvidia announced that **Jensen Huang has resigned as President, CEO and a member of the board**, ending 33 years leading the company he co-founded in 1993
- **Colette Kress**, Nvidia's CFO, is **interim CEO** while the board searches for a permanent successor
- **Harvey C. Jones**, a director since 1993, has been named the board's **first-ever chair**
- Nvidia **did not give a reason** for the departure. The board has announced an independent investigation into a separate matter and said it has no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised
- Nvidia's shares have fallen for six straight weeks and the company closed below a $4 trillion market value on 13 November

This post covers only what has been officially announced and what major outlets have reported on the record. The situation is still developing and the post may be updated.

## What Nvidia Announced

According to Nvidia's announcement, as reported by [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/12/why-did-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resign-and-who-is-colette-kress) and [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/11/nvidia-oust-jensen-huang-spy-affairs/):

- The board accepted Huang's resignation as President, CEO and director
- CFO **Colette Kress** will take over as interim CEO
- The board is searching for a permanent CEO and is considering internal and external candidates
- **Harvey C. Jones**, who has served on the board since 1993, becomes the first chair in the company's history. Before this, Fortune reports, the board had no chair

The board praised Huang's "immeasurable contributions to the company and the industry." It did not state a reason for his resignation.

## The Board's Investigation

Separately, Nvidia's board announced an independent investigation into reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort. Huang said he supported it. The board has stated that it has "no reason to believe that either national security or NVIDIA secrets were compromised."

The investigation has not reported any findings. This post does not repeat press claims about individuals, because none have been confirmed by Nvidia or the board and the investigation is ongoing. The sources below cover the wider reporting.

## About Colette Kress

Kress has been Nvidia's CFO since September 2013. According to Al Jazeera, annual revenue rose from $4.13 billion in fiscal 2014 to $215.9 billion in fiscal 2026 over that period. Before Nvidia she held senior finance roles at Texas Instruments, Microsoft and Cisco. She has said she is committed to building on the foundation Huang created.

## The Market Reaction

According to [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-nvda-stock-jensen-huang-transition.html), Nvidia shares fell 2.01% on Friday 13 November, closing below a $4 trillion market value for the first time since July 2025. The stock was down 12.75% over the week, its sixth consecutive weekly decline and its longest such streak since 2012. CNBC also reports that several analysts had said, before the resignation, that Huang's departure could cost the company as much as 20% of its market value.

## Huang's Career at Nvidia

Huang co-founded Nvidia in 1993, when it was a graphics chip company. Over three decades it became the dominant supplier of the chips and software, notably the CUDA platform, that much of modern AI is trained and run on.

## What Happens Next

These are the open questions as of today:

- Who the permanent CEO will be, and when the board will decide
- What the independent investigation concludes, and when
- How customers, partners and investors respond to a period of interim leadership
